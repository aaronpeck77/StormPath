fix(web): never show NWS polygons without a route or when NWS is off

Remove the raw stormMapGeoJson fallback from nwsAlertGeoJsonForMap. That
fallback was returning the full regional browse-viewport dataset onto the
map whenever no route-specific polygons were found, which showed unrelated
NWS polygons on the map in browse mode and with NWS toggled off.

Now the two hard gates (NWS setting off, no route) are enforced strictly
with no fallback paths around them. Polygons only appear when NWS is on,
a route is active, and at least one alert polygon actually intersects it.
