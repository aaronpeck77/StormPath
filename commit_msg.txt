fix(web): advisory only shows alerts that actually intersect the route

nwsAlertsAffectingActiveRoute was falling back to all corridor alerts when
no route was set, and to all corridor-alerts-with-geometry when the strict
intersection test returned nothing. Both paths caused unrelated nearby alerts
to appear in the advisory panel and hazard timeline as if they were on the route.

Now returns empty when there is no route, and strictly the intersecting alerts
when a route exists. The map polygon display retains its own independent
fallback in stormMapGeoJsonForMap so polygons still render on the map for
alerts whose polygons are thin enough to be missed by sparse route sampling.
